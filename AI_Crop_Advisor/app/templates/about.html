About This Project

AI-Based Weather and Soil Crop Advisor with Plant Disease Detection

University ML Final Project

This system integrates three independently trained machine learning models to provide agriculture decision support. All predictions are from real models with documented datasets, preprocessing, and evaluation metrics.

ML Models

Crop Recommendation Random Forest (F1 = 99.5%)
Plant Disease Detection MobileNetV2 Transfer Learning
Weather Disease Risk SVM (F1 = 80.1%)

Datasets

Crop Recommendation atharvaingle/crop-recommendation-dataset (2,200 rows)
Plant Disease emmarex/plantdisease — PlantVillage (20,638 images)
Rice Disease anshulm257/rice-disease-dataset (3,829 images)
Important Limitation: This system is for educational purposes. Predictions are model estimates and should not substitute professional agricultural advice. Weather risk prediction is specific to rice diseases in the training dataset.
